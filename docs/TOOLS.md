# Tools and assets

Oct 17, 2026

## The cheapest pipeline

You can make every asset in ORION for $0 with free tools. The one purchase worth considering is Aseprite at $19.99, and AI art tools are optional, limited and risky for a hackathon.

| Asset | Cheapest way | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pixel art editor | Pixelorama or LibreSprite; or Aseprite, the industry standard | $0, or $19.99 for Aseprite |
| Cedalion and the guards | Draw by hand at 16×24 px on one shared rig, with armour tiers as palette swaps | $0 |
| Temple kit, tiles, props | Draw from a few base shapes; optionally model columns and steps in MagicaVoxel and clean up the renders | $0 |
| Greybox placeholders | Kenney's isometric packs (CC0) | $0 |
| Normal maps | Laigter | $0 |
| Light textures, palette-swap tables, sprite-sheet import scripts | Generated by script with Claude | $0 |
| UI and vase-painting cards | Drawn in the pixel editor, with a free pixel font | $0 |
| Music | BeepBox or JummBox in the browser, or LMMS with free sampled instruments | $0 |
| Sound effects | jsfxr or ChipTone, plus the free Sonniss GDC bundles and CC0 sounds from Freesound | $0 |
| Concept and mood images | Free tiers of general AI image tools, as reference only, never shipped | $0 |
| **Total** |  | **$0, or about $20 with Aseprite** |

The hackathon judges "it's yours", so the final art should be drawn by you. Free libraries and AI images are for greyboxing and reference, and anything outside that ships gets credited.

## Pixel art editors

Pixelorama is the best free choice; Aseprite is worth its $19.99 mainly because a free Godot plugin imports its files straight into animations.

| Tool | Price | Platforms | Best for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Aseprite](https://steambase.io/games/aseprite/price) | $19.99 on Steam; free if you compile it from source yourself | Windows, macOS, Linux | The industry standard: animation timeline, onion skin, tilemap layers, scripting, command-line export | The free Godot plugin *Aseprite Wizard* imports .aseprite files directly as animations, which saves hours |
| Pixelorama | Free, open source | Windows, macOS, Linux, browser | Close to Aseprite and built with Godot; animation and tilemap mode | Exports PNG sprite sheets; Claude can write the Godot import script |
| LibreSprite | Free, open source | Windows, macOS, Linux | A fork of the old free Aseprite with a familiar layout | Older feature set |
| Piskel | Free, in the browser | Browser | Quick sprite and animation tests | Too basic for the whole game |
| Krita | Free, open source | Windows, macOS, Linux | Painted concept art and the vase-painting cards | Not made for pixel animation |
| Tiled | Free | Windows, macOS, Linux | Isometric map editing | Not needed: Godot's own tile editor covers it |

Both of you should use the same editor so files and palettes move between you without converting.

## Shortcuts for animation and the temple kit

Rendering 3D models into pixel sprites can replace most of the roughly 250 animation frames, but it only pays off if one of you already knows Blender. At 16×24 px, drawing by hand is often just as fast.

| Tool | Price | What it does for ORION | Catch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blender | Free, open source | Model a simple low-poly Cedalion and guard, animate them, and render every frame at the isometric angle at low resolution, then clean up by hand | About 6–10 hours to set up the camera, toon shading and pixelation if you're new to it |
| [Mixamo](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/free-character-animations-rigging) | Free with an Adobe account; royalty-free animations | Auto-rigs a humanoid model and gives you walk, run, attack and climb animations | Humanoids only; in maintenance mode, and it had a multi-day outage in June 2025 |
| [Quaternius](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/free-character-animations-rigging) | Free, CC0 | Low-poly characters and a universal animation library | Backup if Mixamo is down |
| AccuRIG 2.0 | Free (Windows) | Another auto-rigger | Windows only |
| MagicaVoxel | Free | Build columns, steps, statues and fountains as voxels and render them at an isometric angle | Voxel renders need cleanup to match the 2:1 pixel grid |

Whatever you render, finish every frame by hand in the pixel editor with the ORION palette. That last pass is what makes renders look consistent, and what makes the art yours.

## AI art tools

AI can't give you finished ORION art for free: the pixel-art tools are paid after a small trial, and free general tools don't produce clean pixel art. Use AI for concept sketches and reference; if your rules allow it, one month of PixelLab is the best fit.

| Tool | What it makes | Free | Paid | Licence notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [PixelLab](https://www.pixellab.ai/) | Pixel characters with 4 or 8 directions, skeleton animation, isometric support | About 40 trial generations | $12/month (2,000 generations), $24, $50 (10,000) | Check the commercial terms for your tier |
| [Retro Diffusion](https://astropulse.itch.io/retrodiffusion) | Pixel art and tiling textures inside Aseprite | 50 web credits on signup | Aseprite extension: $20 Lite or $65 full, one-time; runs on your own GPU | Extension output runs locally and is yours |
| Scenario | Game art from a custom-trained model | 50 credits/day | From $15/month | Free-tier output can't ship in a released game |
| Leonardo | General images | 150 tokens/day | From $12/month | Free tier is non-commercial |
| Gemini, ChatGPT, Bing Image Creator | General images | Small daily limits that change often | — | Fine for mood boards; not pixel-accurate |

Prices are from a [September 2026 comparison](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/ai-pixel-art-generators) and the vendors' pages; they change often, so check before paying.

### Before you use any of them

- **Check the hackathon rules.** Some ban AI assets or require you to disclose them. The judges also score "it's yours".
- **Expect heavy cleanup.** AI pixel art rarely sits on a clean pixel grid, keeps one palette, or holds the same isometric angle across many images. The cleanup often eats the time you hoped to save.
- **Free tiers are often non-commercial.** That's fine for a hackathon prototype, but not if you ever sell the game.
- **Best uses:** pose references for animation, quick concept sketches of rooms, and palette experiments. Never ship the output as it comes.

## Free asset libraries

Free libraries are great for greyboxing but won't match ORION's look, so treat them as placeholders. CC0 means no credit is needed; anything else, read the licence and credit it.

| Source | What's there | Licence | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Kenney](https://kenney.itch.io/kenney-isometric-assets) | Hundreds of isometric tiles, plus UI and audio packs | CC0; pay what you want, $0 allowed | Greybox placeholders. Its isometric angle and 256×512 tile size differ from ORION's 32×16 grid, so it can't be final art |
| [Quaternius](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/free-character-animations-rigging) | Low-poly 3D characters and animations | CC0 | The Blender render pipeline |
| OpenGameArt.org | Community art, sprites and sound of every kind | Mixed: CC0, CC-BY, GPL and more | Placeholders and sound effects; check each item |
| itch.io free game assets | A huge range of packs | Each pack has its own licence | Placeholders; read every licence |
| game-icons.net | Thousands of simple icons | CC BY 3.0, credit required | Reference for UI icons |
| Google Fonts (Pixelify Sans, Silkscreen, Press Start 2P) | Pixel fonts | SIL Open Font License | UI text you can ship |

## Audio tools and free sound libraries

All of ORION's audio can be made for $0. Compose the music yourselves in a free tool, and build the sound effects from generators, free professional libraries and your own recordings.

| Tool or library | Price | Use it for | Licence notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BeepBox, JummBox | Free, in the browser | Chiptune music in D Dorian and D Phrygian | Your compositions are yours |
| LMMS | Free, open source | A full music program for the layered stems | — |
| Bosca Ceoil | Free | The simplest music maker, for beginners | — |
| Decent Sampler + Pianobook | Free | Sampled harps, flutes and drums to stand in for lyre, aulos and frame drum | Check each instrument's terms |
| jsfxr, ChipTone | Free, in the browser | Retro sound effects: hits, coins, sparks, UI | — |

| Kenney audio packs | Free | UI clicks and impacts | CC0 |
| Your phone | Free | Foley: keys for armour, a flowerpot for pottery, celery for hits, a match for the torch | Yours |

The [2026 Sonniss bundle](https://rekkerd.org/sonniss-releases-gdc-2026-game-audio-bundle/) adds about 7.5 GB from 17 vendors on top of the archive of earlier years.

## Helpers

A few small free tools and scripts save hours of repetitive work.

| Helper | Price | What it saves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Laigter](https://azagaya.itch.io/laigter) | Free, open source (GPL-3.0); pay what you want | Normal maps for marble and walls from your sprites, with a batch mode for many files |
| Lospec | Free | Palette library and palette tools for building the 32-colour palette |
| Aseprite Wizard (Godot plugin) | Free | Imports .aseprite files straight into Godot animations; needs Aseprite |
| OBS Studio | Free, open source | Recording the 90-second backup demo video |
| Claude, by script | Included in your plans | Banded light textures, palette-swap tables, sprite-sheet import, grey-box placeholder tiles, placeholder sound effects |
