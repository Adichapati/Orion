# ORION v1 — overnight polish

Polish pass on the `v1-prototype` branch: real character sprites, game feel, atmosphere, UI,
sound and tuning. The checklist is in priority order; the dated log is at the bottom.

## Morning summary (2026-10-19, session stopped at 80% of the 5-hour window)

**What changed** (8 commits, all tests pass: 118):

1. **Real character sprites.** `tools/process_generated.py` turns the six ChatGPT sheets in
   `art/generated/` into 11 palette-true sprites in `art/final/` (Cedalion, Watchman, Spearman,
   Hoplite front and back; Phineus; Shade). No sheet had animation frames, so characters get
   procedural life instead: walk bob, step squash, breathing, squash and stretch, blob shadows.
2. **Game feel.** Input buffering and attack chains, acceleration, trauma screen shake, hit-stop,
   white hit flashes, knockback, a stagger shudder and dazed stars, toppling KOs with dust, a
   perfect-parry beat (freeze, bell ring, zoom punch, slow motion), camera look-ahead, obols that
   drift to you and chime higher in a streak. A "Screen shake" toggle is in the pause menu.
3. **Atmosphere.** A night sky behind the open-air rooms with a moon and Orion, who sinks toward
   the horizon as the run goes on (the sky is the clock); a dithered vignette; embers from the
   torch, lamps and braziers; dust motes in moonlight; glinting water; redder guard lanterns that
   turn red on alert. The star shrine's black wall notches and the flat-looking portico are fixed.
4. **UI.** An original pixel font made in-repo (`tools/gen_font.py`), room objectives at the top
   of the screen, first-visit control hints per room, an obol counter pop, and a title screen
   that draws Orion line by line.
5. **Sound.** Four footstep takes, a bronze-bell parry ring, an alert stinger, and music layers
   that ease in fast and linger (all generated by `tools/gen_audio.py`).

**Look at first:** `docs/dev/screens/` (six fresh screenshots), then play the colonnade and try
a parry: `godot --path . -- --room=colonnade` (take the sword in the Lion Gate first, or start at
the title).

**Open questions and not done:**

- **The hackathon rules on AI-made assets are unchecked.** The character art is AI-generated.
- Nothing was felt by hand: the buffer (0.15 s), acceleration (26/20 tiles/s²), hit-stop lengths
  and shake amounts are first guesses. Tuning (priority 6) wasn't started.
- Pixel-font sizing: dense text (shop descriptions, objective line, stats) is drawn at 1× and
  everything else at 2×. Check it on the projector; `UiKit.px()` is the one place to change it.
- Where a guard's lantern cone overlaps the torch and moonlight (colonnade), the floor blows out

- The music loops themselves are unchanged; the shop and end card are only restyled by the font;

- Computer use wasn't used (it needs approval, and nobody could give it overnight). Worth

## Notes

- **The character art is AI-generated** (ChatGPT image generation; the source sheets are in
  `art/generated/`). The hackathon's rules on AI-made assets are **still unchecked**: check them
  before the demo, and be ready to say which art is AI-made.
- `tools/process_generated.py` is repeatable: keys out the magenta, crops each view, scales both
  facings of a character by one factor, picks one palette colour per block (the block mean,
  unless a clearly darker group would be lost: outlines, eyes, belts), snaps to
  `art/palette/orion.gpl` in Lab, cleans orphans and holes, re-draws a dark outline, and pastes
  onto the placeholder canvas with the feet on the bottom-centre pixel. Per-sprite settings
  (height, feet position, excluded colours, pixel touch-ups) are in its `JOBS` table.
- The sheets have only a front (SE) and a back (NE) view, no animation frames, so movement is
  procedural (see the log).
